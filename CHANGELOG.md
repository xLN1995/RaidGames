# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added
- Gold Roll: everyone rolls the bet, the lowest roll pays the highest roll the difference.
- Deathroll: players take turns rolling up to the previous result, whoever rolls the 1 pays the player before them.
- Jackpot: the highest roll collects the bet from everyone, with an optional guild bank cut.
- Blackjack without a dealer: cards via /roll 13, "!stand" to stand, closest to 21 collects the bet from everyone.
- Ties are settled by a reroll among the tied players.
- Players join with "!roll" in raid or party chat; only the host needs the addon.
- Leaderboard over all games, "!top x" and "!bottom x" for everyone in the raid.
- English and German, switchable with /rg lang.
- Minimap button and addon compartment entry.
